Hostel Residential Regulations & Student Undertaking

Mandatory standards of conduct for all resident boarders

  • Curfew Adherence: Every student must return to their hostel block before 10:00 PM on weekdays and 10:30 PM on weekends.
  • Zero Tolerance for Ragging: Any form of ragging, intimidation, or physical harassment is a punishable offense under National Anti-Ragging Acts.
  • Noise & Quiet Hours: Mandatory quiet hours commence from 11:00 PM to 06:00 AM. Loud music and disruptions in corridors are prohibited.
  • Cleanliness & Waste Segregation: Wet and dry waste must be deposited into the designated floor bins.
  • Energy Conservation: Turn off room lights, fans, and study lamps when leaving the room.
