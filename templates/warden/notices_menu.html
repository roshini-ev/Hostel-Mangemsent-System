Official Code of Conduct & Resident Handbook

Rules enforced by the Chief Warden, Proctorial Board, and Hall Administration

  1. Curfew & Gate Timings: The main hostel entrance gates close strictly at 10:00 PM on weekdays and 10:30 PM on Saturdays. Boarders returning after curfew are registered in the late log and subject to proctorial review.
  2. Room Keys & Security: Residents are strictly responsible for their personal belongings. Duplicating official hostel cylinder keys without written warden approval is prohibited.
  3. Visitor & Guest Policy: Day guests are permitted only in the designated Ground Floor Reception Lounges between 09:00 AM and 08:00 PM. No visitor is permitted into resident living quarters without special warden pass.
  4. Electrical Appliances: Use of high-wattage personal electric heaters, immersion rods, and induction cooktops inside student rooms is strictly prohibited due to campus fire safety guidelines.
  5. Mess Norms: Food served in the residential dining hall must be consumed within the mess premises. Tiffin carriers are permitted only upon presentation of an authorized sick-outpass voucher from the campus medical officer.
